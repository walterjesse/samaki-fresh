About FastCart

Fresh fish from Lake Victoria, delivered to your doorstep with care.

Our Story

FastCart started with a simple mission: to bring the freshest fish from Lake Victoria directly to homes across Kenya. Founded by Mama Akinyi, we've grown from a small family operation to a trusted marketplace connecting local fishermen with families who deserve quality, sustainably-sourced fish.

Every fish we sell is cleaned, dried, and packaged with the same care Mama Akinyi uses for her own family. We believe in fair prices for fishermen and fresh, affordable fish for everyone.
