Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from FastCart.

How fresh is your fish?

Our fish is sourced daily from Lake Victoria and processed within hours of catch. We clean, dry, and package each order to ensure maximum freshness.

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver to Kisumu, Homa Bay, Nairobi, and surrounding areas. Same-day delivery is available within the city limits.

How do I pay?

We accept M-Pesa and Cash on Delivery for your convenience. Simply select your preferred payment method at checkout.

Can I track my order?

Yes! Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive tracking information via SMS or email so you can monitor your delivery in real-time.

What if I'm not satisfied with my order?

Customer satisfaction is our priority. If you're not happy with your order, please contact us within 24 hours and we'll make it right.

How do I become a seller on FastCart?

We're always looking to partner with quality fishermen and vendors. Contact us through our seller registration page to learn more about joining our marketplace.
