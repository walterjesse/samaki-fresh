Cooking Tips

🐟 Selecting Fresh Fish

Look for clear, bright eyes, firm flesh, and a fresh ocean smell. Our FastCart fish arrives fresh daily from Lake Victoria.

❄️ Storage Tips

Keep fish in the coldest part of your fridge (32-35°F) and use within 1-2 days. For longer storage, freeze immediately.

🔥 Cooking Temperature

Fish is done when it flakes easily with a fork and reaches 145°F internally. Don't overcook - it dries out quickly.

🧂 Seasoning

Keep it simple - fresh fish needs minimal seasoning. Lemon, garlic, salt, and pepper bring out the natural flavors beautifully.
